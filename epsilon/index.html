EPSILON

ἐ ψιλόν · [e]

The 5th letter of the Greek alphabet.

MATHEMATICS

  • A very small quantity (ε).
  • Limits: ε–δ definitions.

PROBABILITY

  • Small errors/perturbations in approximations.
  • Margin ε in inequalities/limits.

STATISTICS

  • Error/residual (ε) in regression.
  • Numerical precision (machine epsilon).
