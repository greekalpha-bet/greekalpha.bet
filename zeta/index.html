ZETA

ζῆτα · [dz]

The 6th letter of the Greek alphabet.

MATHEMATICS

  • Riemann zeta function ζ(s).
  • Occurs in series and number theory.

PROBABILITY

  • Less common; appears in special functions.
  • Sometimes used as a parameter/constant in specific models.

STATISTICS

  • Rare as a standard; used in some contexts as auxiliary parameter.
  • If used, it's author-specific convention.

HOW TO WRITE IT — click to copy

lowercase
uppercasesame as the Latin letter: LaTeX has no command for it

DON'T CONFUSE IT

  • ζ ξ ς γ ζ has a single turn before the tail; ξ has two; ς is the final sigma.
  • ξ ζ ε ς ξ has two turns stacked before the tail — like a 3 with a tail; ζ has only one.

Note: notations vary between books/fields; these are the most common usages.
