CHI

χεῖ · [kʰ]

The 22nd letter of the Greek alphabet.

MATHEMATICS

  • Letter used in special functions.
  • Index/variable in some areas.

PROBABILITY

  • Chi-square distribution χ².
  • Appears in tests and distance measures.

STATISTICS

  • χ² tests (goodness-of-fit/independence).
  • χ² statistic in contingency tables.

PRONUNCIATION

Ancient Greek
sound [kʰ]
Modern Greek
χι [çi] · sound [x] (before e/i: [ç])

IN FORMULAS

  • χ² = Σ (O − E)² / EThe chi-square statistic: observed against expected counts.See it explained at morrisonkuhlsen.com →

HOW TO WRITE IT — click to copy

lowercase
uppercasesame as the Latin letter: LaTeX has no command for it

DON'T CONFUSE IT

  • χ x κ λ χ drops below the baseline and its strokes curve; the Latin x sits on the line.

Note: notations vary between books/fields; these are the most common usages.
