PI

πεῖ · [p]

The 16th letter of the Greek alphabet.

MATHEMATICS

  • Constant π.
  • Product operator Π (uppercase Pi).

PROBABILITY

  • Appears in densities/normalizations (π).
  • In Bayes, π(θ) may denote a prior.

STATISTICS

  • May denote proportion/probability (π).
  • In logistic: π(x)=P(Y=1|x).
