XI

ξεῖ · [ks]

The 14th letter of the Greek alphabet.

MATHEMATICS

  • Variable/index (ξ).
  • Appears in PDEs and special functions.

PROBABILITY

  • Random variable (ξ) in some texts.
  • Parameter in stochastic models (convention).

STATISTICS

  • Sometimes used for latent observations/noise.
  • Depends on author convention.

DON'T CONFUSE IT

  • ζ ξ ς γ ζ has a single turn before the tail; ξ has two; ς is the final sigma.
  • ξ ζ ε ς ξ has two turns stacked before the tail — like a 3 with a tail; ζ has only one.

Note: notations vary between books/fields; these are the most common usages.
