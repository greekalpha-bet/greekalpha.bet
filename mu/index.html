MU

μῦ · [m]

The 12th letter of the Greek alphabet.

MATHEMATICS

  • Constant/index; micro prefix (μ) in units.

PROBABILITY

  • Location parameter (μ) in Normal and other families.
  • Theoretical mean when it exists.

STATISTICS

  • Population mean (μ).
  • Estimate: μ̂ (mu-hat) in some texts.

PRONUNCIATION
