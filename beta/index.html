BETA

βῆτα · [b]

The 2nd letter of the Greek alphabet.

MATHEMATICS

  • Beta function B(·,·) and integrals.
  • Generic constants/coefficients.

PROBABILITY

  • Beta distribution (parameters α, β).
  • Prior/hyperparameter in Bayesian models.

STATISTICS

  • Regression coefficients (β).
  • Type II error (β) and power (1−β).

IN FORMULAS

  • poder = 1 − ββ is the Type II error rate; 1 − β, the power of a test.See it explained at morrisonkuhlsen.com →
  • y = β₀ + β₁x + εIntercept and slope of a linear regression.See it explained at morrisonkuhlsen.com →
