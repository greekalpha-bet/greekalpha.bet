SIGMA

σίγμα · [s]

The 18th letter of the Greek alphabet.

MATHEMATICS

  • Summation Σ (uppercase sigma).
  • Structures: σ-algebra (sigma).

PROBABILITY

  • Probability spaces: σ-algebra (F).
  • σ(X) = σ-algebra generated by X.

STATISTICS

  • Population standard deviation (σ).
  • Variance (σ²) in normal models.

IN FORMULAS

  • Z = (X − μ) / σσ, the population standard deviation, is the unit of the z-score.See it explained at morrisonkuhlsen.com →
  • Σ xᵢ = x₁ + x₂ + … + xₙThe uppercase Σ is the summation operator.

DON'T CONFUSE IT

  • ∑ Σ Ε ∏ ∑ (U+2211, \sum) is the operator, larger and with more air; Σ (U+03A3) is the letter. Copied as text, they are different characters.
