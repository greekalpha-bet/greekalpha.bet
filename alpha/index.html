ALPHA

ἄλφα · [a]

The 1st letter of the Greek alphabet.

MATHEMATICS

  • Angle/constant in formulas.
  • Parameter in functions and series.

PROBABILITY

  • Shape parameter in Beta/Gamma distributions (common convention).
  • Significance level in hypothesis tests (α).

STATISTICS

  • Significance level (α) and Type I error.
  • Can appear as a model/ hyper-parameter.

PRONUNCIATION

Ancient Greek
sound [a]
Modern Greek
